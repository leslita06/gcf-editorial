Galerie Cécile Fakhoury at the 16th Dakar Biennale

19 November – 19 December 2026Dakar, Senegal
For immediate release
Abidjan, 17 November 2026

From 19 November to 19 December 2026, three artists with whom the gallery has collaborated take part in the official international exhibition of the 16th Dak'Art Biennale of Contemporary African Art. Alongside, the gallery presents Lu Nëbtu, a solo exhibition by Kassou Seydou at the Sokhamon.

As part of the official International Exhibition of the 16th edition of the Dak'Art Biennale of Contemporary African Art, Galerie Cécile Fakhoury announces the participation of Serigne Ibrahima Dieye, Cheikh Ndiaye and Eva Diallo, with whom the gallery has previously collaborated.

Titled (Anti)fragility: arts of repair and strategies of resilience, the Biennale is curated by Morad Montazami.

Lu Nëbtu, Kassou Seydou at the Sokhamon

On the occasion of the Biennale, the gallery renews its collaboration with the Sokhamon and presents Lu Nëbtu, a solo exhibition by Kassou Seydou. For this in situ project, the artist extends his pictorial universe into space, giving rise to a forest of canvases and installations. “Lu nëbtu”, “what is hidden” in Wolof, echoes the meaning held by the forest in the artist's native Casamance, as a form of resistance to the levelling of a contemporary world cut off from its roots.

For the first time, the lines of writing Kassou Seydou inscribes in his paintings form beings freed from the canvas, populating a three-dimensional world. The exhibition is part of a programme of three exhibitions bringing together four artists from Senegal, Gabon and France around the theme “Repair(s) of our relations to the world”.

Curated by Galerie Cécile Fakhoury and Margaux Schramm Decroix for the Sokhamon.

A gallery between Abidjan, Dakar and Paris

Opened in Abidjan in September 2012, Galerie Cécile Fakhoury inaugurated a space in Dakar and a showroom in Paris in 2018. It works to promote contemporary art on the African continent through solo and group exhibitions, international art fairs and biennales, and collaborations with galleries abroad.
